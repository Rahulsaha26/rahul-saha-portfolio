ABOUT ME

Turning spatial data into useful decisions.

I am Rahul Saha, a Junior GIS Expert working as a National Consultant – Jr. GIS Expert with the Urban Development Directorate (UDD). My work combines GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, cartography and urban/rural planning.

I work with satellite imagery, administrative and socio-economic datasets, terrain information and planning layers to create maps, analysis and decision-support outputs for development planning.
